# References

## Statistics of the text

- **Bennett, W. R. (1976).** *Scientific and Engineering Problem-Solving with the Computer.* Prentice-Hall. — first report that Voynichese conditional character entropy is far below that of European plain text and of ciphers.
- **Landini, G. (2001).** Evidence of linguistic structure in the Voynich manuscript using spectral analysis. *Cryptologia* 25(4), 275–295. — Zipf's law, entropy, token correlation, long-range structure.
- **Reddy, S. & Knight, K. (2011).** What we know about the Voynich manuscript. *Proceedings of the 5th ACL-HLT Workshop on Language Technology for Cultural Heritage, Social Sciences, and Humanities*, 78–86. — word-length distributions, HMM analysis, morphological segmentation, the abjad argument.
- **Lindemann, L. & Bowern, C. (2020).** Character entropy in modern and historical texts: comparison metrics for an undeciphered manuscript. arXiv:2010.14697. — h2 = 1.91–2.56 for Voynichese against 2.77–6.14 for 316 comparison texts; concentration of high-probability bigrams.
- **Bowern, C. & Lindemann, L. (2021).** The linguistics of the Voynich manuscript. *Annual Review of Linguistics* 7, 285–308. — survey.
- **Amancio, D. et al. (2013).** A systematic comparison of the statistical properties of the Voynich manuscript and natural languages. *PLOS ONE* 8(6). — vocabulary size, word-frequency networks.
- **Montemurro, M. & Zanette, D. (2013).** Keywords and co-occurrence patterns in the Voynich manuscript. *PLOS ONE* 8(6). — information-theoretic topic structure.
- **Shi, Y. & Roush, J. (2014).** *Cracking the Voynich Code 2015 — Final Report.* University of Adelaide. — Zipf, word length, entropy, n-grams, collocations.
- **Stolfi, J.** *Properties of the Voynich Manuscript, Part 1: Book Description and Word Statistics.* Technical report, UNICAMP.
- **Rugg, G. & Taylor, G. (2017).** Hoaxing statistical features of the Voynich manuscript. *Cryptologia* 41(3). — grid-like structure, mechanical generation.
- **Ponnaluri, R. V. (2024).** The Voynich Manuscript was written in a single, natural language. *Cryptologia* 49(6), 505–524. — Zipf, Brevity and Heaps laws; arguments against both the two-language and the five-scribe hypotheses.
- **Greshko, M. (2025).** The Naibbe cipher. *Cryptologia*. — a constructed verbose cipher tested against Voynich statistics.

## Generative and hoax hypotheses

- **Timm, T. & Schinner, A. (2019/2020).** A possible generating algorithm of the Voynich manuscript. *Cryptologia* 44(1), 1–19. — self-citation with copy-and-modify; the generator used as a baseline here.
- **Timm, T. & Schinner, A. (2024).** The Voynich manuscript: discussion of text creation hypotheses. *Cryptologia* 48(4), 305–322.
- **Timm, T. (2019).** [SelfCitationTextgenerator](https://github.com/TorstenTimm/SelfCitationTextgenerator). — executable implementation.

## Handwriting, scribes, palaeography

- **Carter, A. H. (1942).** Handwriting assessment, reported in Stolfi's technical report: the whole text by one person, probably the same one who drew the figures.
- **Currier, P. (1976).** Papers on the Voynich manuscript. — two hands, "Language A" and "Language B".
- **Davis, L. F. (2020).** How many glyphs and how many scribes? Digital palaeography and the Voynich manuscript. *Manuscript Studies* 5(1). — five scribes; diagnostics in the `<k>` gallows and the word-final `<n>` flourish; work divided by bifolium rather than by page.
- **Farrugia, K., Layfield, C. & van der Plas, L. (2022).** Demystifying the scribes behind the Voynich manuscript using computational linguistic techniques. *CEUR-WS* Vol-3313. — character n-gram authorship attribution.
- **One Hand, Five Labels: A Critical Examination of the Five-Scribe Hypothesis** (Zenodo 18864426). — argues the diagnostic features occur on nearly every page, that the five hands reduce to two, and that the chain of confirmation is circular.

## Physical evidence

- **University of Arizona AMS (2009).** Radiocarbon dating of four vellum samples: **1404–1438** at ~95 % confidence. Alternative summaries in the conference literature give the 1450s.
- **McCrone Associates (2009), J. B. Barabe.** Vellum; iron-gall ink; azurite; copper resinate; gum. Nothing modern or synthetic. Note: pigments were assessed for *compatibility with the period*, not dated; the report does not date the ink.
- **Tucker, A. & Talbert, R. (2014).** A preliminary analysis of the botany, zoology and mineralogy of the Voynich manuscript. *HerbalGram* 106. — proposes a colonial New Spain context, 1521–1576, and reports atacamite in the green paint. Contradicted by the radiocarbon date.
- Cyan/IR imaging and XRF: the alphabets on f. 1r carry a zinc signature and are later additions.

## Transcriptions and corpora

- [voynich.nu/data](https://www.voynich.nu/data/) — IVTFF 2.0 transcriptions: Takahashi (EVA), Currier (v101), Zandbergen–Landini, FSG, Friedman. The basis of every measurement here.
- [Universal Dependencies](https://universaldependencies.org/) — the thirteen reference corpora.
- [Voynich Manuscript, Beinecke MS 408](https://collections.library.yale.edu/catalog/2002046) — IIIF images.
