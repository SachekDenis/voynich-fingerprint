THE SCRIBE'S MANUAL
==================================================================

Everything needed to write text of this kind by hand. No counting,
no arithmetic: each rule below is one look-up and one roll of a die.

G.  STOCK PHRASES -- after a word that has a habitual partner, write the
    partner instead of thinking of a new word: 30% of the time.

H.  PAGE RULES
      fill the column that the drawing leaves and no more
      a passage runs a few lines; when it ends, start a fresh one and
        take your forms from that passage, not from the page before
      open a line with a tall stroke now and then
      now and then write a word again from the same passage with one
        shape swapped for the one that usually follows it (12%)
      tall stroke at a line opening, about 8% of lines

PROCEDURE for one word
      1. glance at the last letter you wrote -> onset class (F)
      2. write the opening (B)
      3. decide how long this word is (D) and how it ends (E)
      4. fill the middle with shapes (C) until the word is that size
      5. if a line is already running, keep the word short enough to
         reach the margin without running past it
